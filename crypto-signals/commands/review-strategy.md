# /review-strategy

Review the Aswad Crypto Signals strategy as a quantitative research task.

Assess:
- Whether the breakout and retest logic uses only information available at the signal candle close.
- Whether support/resistance levels are calculated from prior candles only.
- Candle-close confirmation and relative-volume calculation.
- Stop placement, target calculation and reward-to-risk consistency.
- Exchange-specific market availability, fees, slippage and liquidity.
- False breakouts, regime sensitivity and correlated exposures.
- Backtest design, out-of-sample validation, walk-forward testing and overfitting risk.

Do not label the strategy profitable based on a small sample or in-sample results. Recommend tests and measurable acceptance criteria before any reliance on live alerts. Do not add automatic execution.
